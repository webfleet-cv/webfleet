# Webfleet clustering review boundary

Independent review should scrutinize: every site/group/tag mutation bypass; follower forwarding and caller operation identity; semantic conflicts versus replica-health failures; snapshot reconciliation preserving node-local monitoring history; clean-fleet bootstrap; full-mesh Gantry trust versus Raft membership; operator authorization; shutdown ordering; and the explicit exclusion of analytics/monitoring/audit/webhook side effects from consensus.

The implementation is not considered real-infrastructure certified merely because Watchpost and Trestle are. Webfleet needs its own independent review and dogfood campaign after local gates pass.
